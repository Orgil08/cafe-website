Our Story

A Gathering Place Built on Craft & Warmth

Aura was founded in 2021 with an earnest idea: that a neighborhood café should feel like an extension of your own living room—filled with good smells, honest ingredients, unpretentious hospitality, and a cup of coffee that genuinely brightens your morning.

Whether you need a quiet corner to write your thesis, a cozy booth to catch up with old friends, or a quick energizing flat white on your commute, we have a seat saved for you.
